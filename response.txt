## Changes Made
1. **Observability Engine**: Created `sim/observability/logger.py` to capture and analyze connector decisions.
2. **Explainable AI**: Connectors now return a "reason" string for every node choice, allowing us to trace outcomes back to specific traits.
3. **Trait Influence Tracking**: The simulation now identifies which personality trait (Aggression, Strategy, etc.) was most active during the run.
4. **Economic Forensics**: Added tracking for the "Top 10 Most Expensive Decisions" to find price spikes and their causes.

---

## Results
- **Most Active Trait**: `aggression` (Seed 42).
- **Price Spike Trace**: Found that the most expensive decision was driven by a `random_fallback`, demonstrating the cost of non-strategic movement.

---

## Next Suggestions
1. **Conviction Mechanics**: Now that we can see *why* nodes are picked, we can add "stickiness" for connectors who repeatedly pick the same node for strategic reasons.
2. **Node Discounts**: Allow nodes to actively reduce costs for connectors with high conviction.
3. **Threshold Alerts**: Add warnings when a single decision consumes more than 50% of a connector's balance.
