## Changes Made
1. **Observability Layer**: Created a dedicated logger in `sim/observability/` that captures the "Who, What, Why, and How Much" of every interaction.
2. **Decision Traceability**: Modified the connector logic to return a reason string (e.g., "aggression", "strategy", "chaos") along with the chosen node.
3. **Enhanced Experiment Metrics**:
   - **Top 10 Expensive Decisions**: Automatically tracks the highest-cost interactions in each run.
   - **Most Influential Trait**: Identifies which personality trait drove the most decisions across the simulation.
4. **Non-Invasive Integration**: Wrapped the existing engine logic to collect logs without altering the core economic mechanics.

---

## Findings
- **Most Influential Trait (Seed 42)**: `aggression`
- **Most Expensive Decision (Seed 42)**: A connection costing 273.7 tokens occurred on Day 22, driven by a `random_fallback` decision. This highlights how even non-strategic moves can be extremely costly in high-pressure states.
