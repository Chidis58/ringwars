# 🎮 RingWars Onboarding System

> Designed to protect new users while teaching the economy through experience—not instruction.

---

## 🧠 Core Principle
Safe → Understand → Compete → Lose → Recover → Enter Chaos

New users should:
- win early
- lose safely
- understand why

---

## 🌱 Phase 1 — Sandbox (0–15 min)

### Environment
- AI Nodes
- AI Connectors
- Subsidized costs

### Goals
- Learn:
  - 🪙 spending
  - 💍 bidding
  - ⤵️ impact

### Rules
- Lower costs
- Minimal penalties
- Easy 💍 wins

---

## 🎯 Phase 2 — Guided Competition

### Add
- 2–3 real players
- Mild cluster pressure

### Objectives
- Hold 💍 briefly
- Win multiple Nodes
- Avoid overlap

---

## 🛡️ Phase 3 — Protection Layer (24–48 hrs)

### 1. Loss Cap
max_loss_per_hour = 30–40%

---

### 2. Discount Shield
- Reduced escalation on early bids
- Partial refunds on losses

---

### 3. Soft Matchmaking
- Low ⤵️ Nodes
- Low competition clusters

---

### 4. Hidden Boost
- Increased 💠 gain early

---

## 💥 Phase 4 — First Loss (Critical Moment)

### When player loses:
- 💍
- or significant 🪙

### Provide:

#### Insight
- “High overlap increased cost”

#### Recovery
- small 🪙 boost
- discounted next move

#### Suggestion
- “Try lower-pressure Nodes”

---

## 🔥 Phase 5 — Full Economy Unlock

### Trigger
- time OR
- actions OR
- skill indicators

### Remove:
- protections
- cost reductions

### Notify:
> “You are now entering the live economy”

---

## 🔴 Node Onboarding

### Early Experience
- Limited incoming requests
- High control

---

### Teach Quickly
- Low ⤵️ = power
- High ⤵️ = risk

---

### First Win
- Trigger small bidding war
- Earn 🪙 early

---

## 🎯 First Session Success Criteria

User should:
- Win at least 1 💍
- Lose at least 1 💍
- Understand cost changes
- Retain some 🪙

---

## ⚠️ Avoid

### ❌ Immediate exposure to:
- whales
- high ⤵️ Nodes
- complex clusters

---

### ❌ Over-tutorializing
Let the system teach through:
- outcomes
- feedback

---

## 🧪 Optional Features

### Ghost Replay
Show:
> “What would have happened if…”

---

## 🧩 Onboarding Loop
Learn → Compete → Lose → Recover → Adapt → Repeat

---

## Philosophy

> Don’t protect users from loss.  
> Protect them from **confusing loss**.

Understanding creates retention.
Confusion creates churn.
